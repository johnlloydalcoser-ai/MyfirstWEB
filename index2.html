Discovering Hilongos

Hilongos is a rich, historical municipality located in the province of Leyte, Philippines. Known for its deep cultural roots and vibrant community, it serves as a bustling center of commerce and faith in the region.

One of the town's most iconic landmarks is the Hilongos Bell Tower. Built during the Spanish colonial period, it stands as a testament to the town's enduring history and the strong spirit of the Hilongosnons.

Whether you are exploring the ancient ruins, participating in the lively Alikaraw Festival, or enjoying the scenic coastal views, Hilongos offers a unique blend of historical preservation and modern local progress. The local government and community continue to work together to keep the town's heritage alive while paving the way for future generations.
